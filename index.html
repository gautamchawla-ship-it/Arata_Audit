Key findings

  • Meta scaled into the MitoActive launch and lost efficiency: spend rose from ₹47.3L in Aug to ₹67.0L in Sep (+42%) while ROAS fell from 2.69x to 2.01x and CPA rose from ₹485 to ₹680. CPM barely moved (₹120 to ₹106), so the decline is conversion efficiency and mix, not auction cost.
  • The best Meta structure was cut while the weakest was scaled. Catalog (DPA) campaigns delivered 3.00x at CPA ₹344 but spend fell from ₹21.8L in Aug to ₹6.9L in Sep; in-house prospecting ran at 1.96x and CPA ₹802 yet spend grew from ₹12.4L to ₹44.8L.
  • Creator (BCA) content is the second largest Meta line at ₹29.9L and 2.19x; it held 2.81x in Aug and fell to 1.88x in Sep as the same Nitin Joshi assets absorbed ₹12.6L. Refresh, not more budget, is the lever.
  • Meta reaches the right people: men are 83% of spend, 18 to 34 is 68%, and 55 plus is 1.4%. 18 to 24 is the cheapest band (CPA ₹489) and 45 to 54 the dearest (₹736). Click to purchase held flat from Aug to Sep; the Sep CPA rise came from CPC (₹14.41 to ₹14.19), not from the site.
  • Over half of Meta purchases are not new customers: existing customers took 14% of spend and produced 26% of purchases, engaged audiences another 27%. The cost of a new customer on Meta is ₹774, not the blended ₹596. Catalog still has the cheapest new-customer CPA (₹537) of any group.
  • Google is a brand-harvesting account. Brand Search alone is ₹63.1L at 13.52x; brand Search plus brand-inclusive PMax account for 81% of non-video spend. Non-brand Search is only ₹76K at 2.02x.
  • ₹60.6L of Google spend (39%) went to YouTube reach and views campaigns that recorded 39 conversions; ₹42.3L of that served on TV screens with 6 conversions. If these were awareness buys, they need a lift measurement and a separate budget line; inside the sales account they halve reported ROAS.
  • Google search terms show the brand campaigns are clean (0.3% on non-brand queries) but brand plus product searches ("arata shampoo", "arata hair serum") cost ₹10.04 a click against ₹3.62 for the brand name, and their CPC rose from ₹6.11 in Jun to ₹15.60 in Oct. Misspelled brand searches are being matched at seven times the brand CPC.
  • Brand Search is growing in cost faster than in value. Jan to Sep 2026 against Jan to Sep 2025: spend +48% (₹20.5L to ₹30.4L), value +13%, ROAS 16.26x to 12.37x, CVR 12.0% to 10.4%. Clicks on the keyword "arata" rose 23% but its conversions were flat (28,620 to 28,458): brand demand is up, brand conversion rate is down.
  • Standard Shopping (₹5.7L, 3.93x) ran from Nov 2025 to Mar 2026 and has been dormant since April; while live it sat on the same feed and product families as non-brand PMax (₹9.6L, 4.87x) and brand PMax (₹14.5L, 8.85x); anti-dandruff shampoo alone had ₹2.9L in Shopping competing with ₹2.2L in PMax.

What the next exports would unlock

  • Google campaign report by month without any segment (no ad group, keyword or product): the only way to put Performance Max and the YouTube reach campaigns on the monthly view. Search, Shopping and Demand Gen are now monthly; PMax and YouTube are not.
  • Google Auction insights for the IHGS, Super Shampoo and Mito brand campaigns: who is bidding on brand plus product and misspelled brand queries.
  • Performance Max search terms (insights tab): how much of brand PMax value comes from brand queries.
  • Google change history: budget, target ROAS, brand exclusion and conversion action changes, measured 28 days before and after.
  • Meta ad level export from Jan 2025: a pre-launch baseline for the launch comparison.
  • Meta funnel counts (landing page views, adds to cart, checkouts as numbers rather than ratios): the click to purchase funnel.

Campaign structure

  • In-house prospecting (TOF campaigns built on statics, motion graphics, founder and creator down-edits with interest and Advantage+ ad sets) is half the account at ₹64.1L (50%) and the weakest large group at 1.96x, CPA ₹802.
  • Catalog (DPA) is ₹28.8L (22%) at 3.00x and CPA ₹344, the lowest CPA in the account by a wide margin. Its AOV is ₹1,032 against ₹1,575 for in-house prospecting, so catalog sells the single 30 ml serum while prospecting sells packs and combos.
  • BOF retargeting (cart and checkout abandoners) is small at ₹4.3L but runs 2.76x; MOF web-visitor retargeting is ₹1.2L at 1.62x and the two doctor-led MOF and BOF campaigns from Sep 11 were under 1x.
  • The Oct 8 creator campaigns from the media plan have ₹14K of spend so far at 2.19x; not enough to judge yet.

Largest and best campaigns inside the groups

  • One campaign, TOF_Mito-Preorder_Conversion_20.07.2026, is 40% of all spend (₹51.4L) at 1.94x and CPA ₹814. Everything else is fragmented across 37 campaigns.
  • The best large campaigns are TOF_Catalog_Mito-Preorder_Conversion_21.08.2026 (Advantage+ catalog prospecting, 4.13x, CPA ₹212 on ₹2.3L) and Catalog_Mito-Preorder_Conversion_31.07.2026 (product-viewer catalog, 3.06x, CPA ₹335 on ₹23.0L). Both are under-funded relative to their results.
  • 6 campaigns with over ₹20,000 spend are below 1.6x, including the Incremental catalog relaunch (Catalog_Mito-Preorder_Conversion_07.09.2026, 1.58x) and the Sep 1 Advantage+ New Cycle campaign (1.58x on ₹6.4L).
  • 9 campaigns carried spend in Oct 1 to 9. The account launched 14 new campaigns in Sep and 14 in Aug; most ran under ₹1L and never left learning.

  • In Aug to Oct 2026, 67% of brand Search spend went on queries that two or more Arata campaigns served in the same month. In Sep the single query "arata" was served by 4 different campaigns, and "arata hair serum" by four.
